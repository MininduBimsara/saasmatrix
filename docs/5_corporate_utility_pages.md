# 5. Corporate Compliance & Supporting Directories

This document outlines the utility, legal, and communication pages that complete the directory ecosystem.

---

## 5.1 Corporate Foundation Pages

### About SaaSMatrix (`/about`)
* **Purpose:** Explains our methodology for reviewing and measuring software. This page builds trust with visitors by documenting our rating standards and independence.
* **Component Layout:** Split-grid sections detailing our mission, analysis framework, and editorial values.

### Contact Portal (`/contact`)
* **Purpose:** Provides a communication hub for SaaS vendors seeking to claim profiles, and business buyers with custom requests.
* **Functional Features:** Includes contact forms with real-time field validation, feedback displays, and email dispatch vectors.

---

## 5.2 Legal & Monetization Disclosures

### AdSense Monetization Disclaimers (`/disclaimers`)
* **Purpose:** Meets compliance requirements for the Google AdSense program. This page provides clear notices regarding third-party tracking, cookies, and paid referrals on SaaSMatrix.
* **Required Disclosures:** Documented guidelines regarding:
  * Google AdSense program participation.
  * Personalized ad delivery via DoubleClick DART cookies.
  * Affiliate marketing relationships and sponsored reviews disclosures.

### Privacy Policy (`/privacy`)
* **Purpose:** Clarifies how SaaSMatrix collects, processes, and protects visitor data, ensuring GDPR and CCPA compliance.
* **Protected Metrics:**
  * Browser cookies and web beacons.
  * Newsletter email registration lists.
  * Integration parameters stored in local storage variables.

### Terms of Service (`/terms`)
* **Purpose:** Outlines the legal terms of use for SaaSMatrix, listing user restrictions, content copyright terms, and warranty disclaimers.
* **Key Clauses:**
  * B2B calculator reports disclaimer (TCO results are approximations).
  * Terms regarding scraping, extraction, or crawling of directory lists.
  * Legal limits of liability on comparison tables of software candidates.
